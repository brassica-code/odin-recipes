Egg Tomato Recipe

Back to Home

Description

This is a simple and delicious egg tomato recipe that combines the flavors of fresh tomatoes and eggs. Perfect for a quick meal!

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tomato
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and whisk them.
  2. Cut the tomato into small pieces.
  3. Heat some oil in a pan and pour in the egg mixture.
  4. Once the eggs are almost set, add the tomato pieces and season with salt and pepper.
  5. Cook for a few more minutes until everything is well combined and heated through.
