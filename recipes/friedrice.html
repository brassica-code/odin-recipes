Fried Rice Recipe

Back to Home

Description

This is a simple and delicious fried rice recipe that's perfect for a quick meal!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and whisk them.
  2. Heat some oil in a pan and pour in the egg mixture.
  3. Cook the eggs until they are almost set.
  4. Add the cooked rice and season with salt and pepper.
  5. Mix everything together until well combined and heated through.
