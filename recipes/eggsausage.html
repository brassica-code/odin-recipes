Egg Sausage Recipe

Back to Home

Description

This is a delicious and filling egg sausage recipe that's perfect for breakfast or brunch.

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 100g sausage meat
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and whisk them.
  2. Cut the sausage into small pieces.
  3. Heat some oil in a pan and pour in the egg mixture.
  4. Once the eggs are almost set, add the sausage pieces and season with salt and pepper.
  5. Cook for a few more minutes until everything is well combined and heated through.
